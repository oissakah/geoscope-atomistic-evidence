# Blinded outside-scientist review packet

This folder contains 96 blind questions, one paired structured/report base case for every one of the 48 selected OC20 source groups. The same physical question may have different *evidence aliases* across the two presentations. Labels and valid proofs are withheld from this review packet.

**Reviewer tasks (without accessing reference):** For each case, report (1) the physical measurement defined by the claim; (2) whether any visible evidence record and permitted mathematical operation conclusively supports or contradicts it; (3) the relevant evidence ID, operation, verdict, and a short justification; (4) uncertainty/conflicts or questionable physics. Check atom subsets, endpoint comparisons, rigid alignment, units, PBC, and whether missing information is genuinely insufficient.

Reviewers should also spot-check the raw coordinates and covalent-radius bond rules in the OC20 source collection, rather than relying solely on the textual proofs. Original OC20 archive identification and checksum appear in the freeze manifest. Keep a record of reviewer identity, expertise, date, and disagreements. Do not read any reference labels until blind judgments are submitted.

Use `EXPERT_REVIEW_BLINDED.jsonl` as the source of all model-input case facts. Add judgments to `expert_response_template.csv` (or update per-case JSON fields) and return it. A separate `SEALED_OFFLINE` answer key exists only in the owner's master freeze. Coordinate arrays for 48 systems are in the accompanying geometry file under the master archive (not in the standalone blind review ZIP), available by request to the owner.
